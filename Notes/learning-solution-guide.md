Learning from a LeetCode solution involves several steps to ensure you
understand the problem-solving approach, the logic behind the solution, and how
you can apply similar techniques to other problems. Here's a structured approach
to learning from a LeetCode solution:

### 1. **Understand the Problem Statement**

- **Read the Problem Carefully:** Make sure you fully understand what the
  problem is asking for. Identify the input, expected output, and any
  constraints.
- **Consider the Edge Cases:** Think about possible edge cases or special cases
  that the solution needs to handle.

### 2. **Attempt the Problem First**

- **Try to Solve It on Your Own:** Before looking at any solution, attempt to
  solve the problem yourself. Even if you don’t arrive at the correct solution,
  this step is crucial for identifying where you might need help.

### 3. **Analyze the Provided Solution**

- **Read Through the Solution:** If you get stuck or after you’ve submitted your
  solution, go through the solutions provided by others or the official
  solution.
- **Understand the Logic:** Focus on the logic used in the solution. Understand
  why certain steps were taken, how the algorithm works, and how it handles
  different cases.
- **Compare with Your Approach:** If you attempted the problem yourself, compare
  your solution with the one provided. Identify the differences and try to
  understand why the provided solution might be more efficient or correct.

### 4. **Break Down the Code**

- **Step-by-Step Analysis:** Break down the solution code line by line. Write
  comments or notes explaining what each part of the code does.
- **Identify Key Concepts:** Recognize any specific data structures, algorithms,
  or programming concepts used in the solution (e.g., dynamic programming,
  greedy algorithms, recursion).

### 5. **Reimplement the Solution**

- **Write the Code Yourself:** Without copying, try to reimplement the solution
  on your own. This reinforces understanding and helps you remember the
  approach.
- **Optimize if Possible:** If the solution is not optimal, consider how it
  could be improved. Implement any optimizations and test them.

### 6. **Reflect on the Learning**

- **Generalize the Solution:** Think about how the solution technique can be
  applied to other problems. Consider what types of problems are similar to the
  one you just solved.
- **Document Your Learnings:** Keep a notebook or digital document where you
  summarize the problem, the solution, and what you learned. This will help you
  review and reinforce your knowledge later.

### 7. **Practice Variations**

- **Solve Similar Problems:** LeetCode often has problems that are variations on
  a theme. Try solving similar problems to solidify your understanding of the
  solution technique.
- **Create Your Own Problems:** If possible, modify the problem slightly (e.g.,
  changing constraints or inputs) and solve it again.

### 8. **Review and Revisit**

- **Periodic Review:** Regularly revisit problems you’ve solved before,
  especially ones that you found challenging. This helps in retaining the
  concepts and techniques.

### 9. **Discuss with Others**

- **Participate in Discussions:** Join LeetCode forums or other coding
  communities to discuss the solution. Explaining the problem and solution to
  others is a great way to reinforce your understanding.

By following these steps, you can effectively learn from LeetCode solutions,
build your problem-solving skills, and apply what you've learned to tackle more
complex problems.
